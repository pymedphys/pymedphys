DICOM Standard

The JSON files in this directory hold tables generated from the DICOM Standard,
edition 2026d: DICOM PS3.3 2026d, © NEMA; DICOM PS3.4 2026d, © NEMA; DICOM
PS3.6 2026d, © NEMA; DICOM PS3.15 2026d, © NEMA; DICOM PS3.16 2026d, © NEMA.
The National Electrical Manufacturers Association (NEMA) publishes the DICOM
Standard at https://www.dicomstandard.org/. The tables are generated by
pymedphys dev deid-tables, never edited by hand, and regenerated for each new
edition of the DICOM Standard.

The tables were extracted automatically from the published DICOM Standard and
may contain errors. The published Standard is authoritative.

Association (NEMA) for its standards publications relating to digital
communications of medical information. NEMA does not endorse, sponsor, or
certify PyMedPhys.
