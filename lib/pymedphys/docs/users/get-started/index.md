or unsure whether you should use the library, the CLI, or the app layer.

1. [What PyMedPhys can do](what-pymedphys-can-do.md)
2. [Choose your path](choose-your-path.md)
3. [Installation options](installation-options.md)
4. [Quick Start Guide](quick-start.rst)

[Using the CLI](cli.rst) after installation.
